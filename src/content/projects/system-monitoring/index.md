*In Progress*

Built end-to-end IoT telemetry platform unifying solar production, battery, and power consumption data streams for an off-grid home. Delivered a working prototype in approximately 2 business days using AI-assisted development — a project previously deferred indefinitely due to estimated effort. Includes real-time alerting and a planned AI prediction layer for generator scheduling based on weather and solar forecasts.

**Tech stack:** Go, React/TypeScript, TimescaleDB, Docker, MQTT, WebSockets
